# CPYOS 晋江支持声明 / JJWXC Support Statement

版本 1.0 · 2026-11-06

CPYOS 是一个非官方、非商业、由开源社区爱好者维护的互操作性项目。我们尊重晋江文学城及其作者、权利人的合法权益，也尊重平台的用户协议。本声明说明本项目如何支持晋江阅读，以及我们主动遵守的边界。

CPYOS is an unofficial, non-commercial interoperability project maintained by open-source hobbyists. We respect JJWXC, its authors and rights holders, and the platform's terms. This statement explains how the project supports JJWXC reading and the limits we impose on ourselves.

## 1. 我们不做什么 / What we do not do

- 不侵入、不攻击、不扫描晋江服务器，不利用漏洞，不绕过登录、验证码、风控或访问控制。
- 不绕过付费：对未购买且未获用户明确授权的 VIP 章节不抓取正文，只写占位；购买走晋江官方购买流程，并在购买前显示价格、要求用户确认（或使用用户明确保存的支付密码）。
- 不提供作品下载站、不做批量抓取、不对外分发正文；不把缓存内容上传到任何服务器。
- 不破解或规避技术保护措施；不修改、伪造或冒用晋江的客户端、签名或身份。

- We do not intrude into, attack or scan JJWXC servers, exploit vulnerabilities, or bypass login, captcha, risk-control or access controls.
- We do not bypass payment: we do not fetch the body of an unpurchased VIP chapter without the user's explicit authorization; unpurchased chapters become placeholders. Purchases use JJWXC's official flow, with the price shown and the user's confirmation (or a payment password the user explicitly saved).
- We do not run a download site, bulk-scrape, redistribute chapter bodies, or upload cached content to any server.
- We do not crack or circumvent technical protection measures, and we do not alter, forge or impersonate JJWXC clients, signatures or identities.

## 2. 我们做什么 / What we do

- 使用用户自己的登录会话，访问该账号本来就有权阅读的内容。
- 为个人阅读提供更好的排版、离线缓存、进度同步和书架管理。
- 对本地缓存和成品 EPUB 使用设备绑定密钥加密；退出登录或删除缓存时清除本地副本。
- 把付费边界写进代码：未购买章节只显示占位，不把“未购买”伪装成“已缓存”。
- 默认不保存密码；用户可显式选择仅保存账号或保存密码，凭据只保存在设备本地，并按项目既有格式混淆存储。

- We use the user's own login session to access content the account is already entitled to read.
- We provide better typography, offline caching, progress sync and shelf management for personal reading.
- Local chapter caches and packaged EPUBs are encrypted with a device-bound key; logging out or deleting the cache removes the local copy.
- The payment boundary is enforced in code: unpurchased chapters are placeholders, never disguised as cached content.
- Passwords are not saved by default; the user may explicitly choose to save only the account or the password. Credentials stay on the device in the project's existing obfuscated format.

## 3. 法律立场 / Legal position

- 在中国法下，个人为学习、研究或者欣赏而使用他人已经发表的作品，存在《著作权法》规定的合理使用空间。本项目只在用户自身账号有权访问的范围内提供阅读便利，不提供规避技术措施、绕过付费或向公众传播作品的能力。
- 本项目不是晋江的官方客户端，与晋江文学城无隶属、合作或背书关系；晋江及相关商标归其权利人所有。
- 本项目不鼓励、也不支持任何侵犯著作权或违反平台协议的行为。用户应自行确保其使用行为合法，并遵守晋江的用户协议。

- Under Chinese law, individual use of a published work for personal study, research or appreciation may fall within the fair-use space provided by the Copyright Law. This project only makes reading more convenient within the content the user's own account is entitled to access; it does not provide the ability to circumvent technical measures, bypass payment, or communicate works to the public.
- This project is not an official JJWXC client and has no affiliation, partnership or endorsement relationship with JJWXC. JJWXC and related marks belong to their respective owners.
- This project does not encourage or support copyright infringement or violations of platform terms. Users are responsible for ensuring that their use is lawful and complies with JJWXC's terms.
